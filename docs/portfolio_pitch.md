# BehaviorGraph — Portfolio Pitch

## One-liner

Behavioral analytics product that turns product events into journeys, activation funnels, retention cohorts, friction signals and opportunity memos.

## Interview talking points

1. Event taxonomy as a product contract (not just logging)
2. Activation funnel vs vanity conversion metrics
3. Retention cohorts and why week-0 definition matters
4. Path graphs as exploratory journey maps (not causal attribution)
5. Friction radar → product opportunity memo with explicit limitations

## Demo path (3 minutes)

1. Open the cockpit with synthetic SaaS onboarding events
2. Walk the activation funnel drop-offs
3. Show journey transitions from the path graph
4. Inspect segments (activated / at-risk / power)
5. Read the opportunity memo and limitations

## Positioning

Not a Mixpanel clone. A portfolio-grade studio for instrumentation thinking, journey analysis and product decision narrative.
